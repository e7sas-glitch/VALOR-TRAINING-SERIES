# Evidence-aware supplement education

## Workflow
- Add educational descriptions only; do not claim treatment or guaranteed results.
- Show common use, relevant precautions, and source/date when evidence is available.
- Do not infer authenticity from barcode, product title, Creapure logo, or photos alone.
- Distinguish general adult reference doses from personal medical advice.
- For pregnancy, kidney disease, medication interactions, or minors, avoid personalized dose and advise clinical review.
- Verify product nutrients and ingredients against label where possible; identify unknowns.
- Keep supplement content separate from workout data and do not alter approved VALOR design.

## Completion
Provide changed file paths, tests performed, remaining issues, and confirmation that the approved workout was not modified.
